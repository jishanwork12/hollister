Why a promotional umbrella keeps advertising long after the campaign ends

Hollister Corporation · 4 min read

Most advertising stops working the day you stop paying for it. A poster comes down, a boosted post ends, a leaflet goes in the bin. An umbrella is different, because the person who receives it keeps using it.

It goes where your customers go

A billboard reaches only the people who pass one road. An umbrella leaves the shop with a dealer, crosses the bazaar, waits at a bus stand and stands open outside an office. Every time it opens, your logo is at eye level for everyone nearby.

People keep it

In Bangladesh an umbrella is used in the rain and in the summer sun, so it is needed for much of the year. A useful gift is not thrown away. That is why companies choose umbrellas for dealer programmes, retailer gifts and field staff.

The print area is large

A pen or a keyring gives your logo a few millimetres. A straight umbrella gives you eight wide panels. You can repeat the logo, add a slogan, or use two brand colours on alternating panels so the umbrella is recognisable from a distance.

What makes it work

  • Use your real brand colours on the canopy, not a random stock colour.
  • Keep the logo big and simple so it reads from across a street.
  • Choose a frame that survives wind. A broken umbrella carries your name too.

If you are planning a campaign, send us your logo and quantity and we will quote for it.

Straight, folding or auto-fold: which umbrella suits your corporate gift

Hollister Corporation · 3 min read

The right umbrella depends on who will carry it. Start with the person, then pick the type.

Straight promotional umbrella

This is the large umbrella you see outside shops and in dealer programmes. It has the biggest print area and is the easiest to notice from far away. Choose it when visibility matters most: retailers, field teams, market campaigns and events.

Folding umbrella

A folding umbrella fits in an office bag, so it suits staff and clients who commute. The canopy is smaller, so keep the logo simple. A UV-coated canopy makes it useful in the summer sun as well as the rain.

Auto-fold umbrella

It opens and closes with one button and feels like a finished product in the hand. Choose it for gifts where the impression matters: senior clients, annual gifts and gift boxes.

A quick way to decide

  • Want to be seen in the market: straight umbrella.
  • Giving to office staff: folding umbrella.
  • Giving to important clients: auto-fold umbrella.

Many companies order two types in one run, one for dealers and one for their own team. Tell us the mix you need and we will quote for both.

Six things to have ready before you order logo-printed umbrellas

Hollister Corporation · 3 min read

An order moves fastest when the first message already has what the factory needs. Keep these six things ready.

1. A clean logo file

Send the original file from your designer: AI, PDF or a large PNG. A logo copied from a Facebook profile picture is small and prints with rough edges.

2. Your brand colours

Decide the canopy colour first. A dark logo needs a light panel and a light logo needs a dark panel. If your brand has two colours, alternating panels show both.

3. Where the logo sits

The logo can go on one panel, on every second panel, or on all of them. More panels means the logo is visible from every side.

4. The quantity

We sell wholesale, so the price depends on how many pieces you take. Give a number even if it is an estimate.

5. Your deadline

Tell us the date of your event or programme at the start. Printing and production take time, and a known deadline lets us tell you honestly whether it can be met.

6. The delivery address

We deliver across Bangladesh. One address or several, say so early so delivery can be planned with the order.

With these six ready, one WhatsApp message is enough to get your quote.

When to order umbrellas for a monsoon campaign

Hollister Corporation · 3 min read

The best time to hand out an umbrella is the week before people need one. That means the order has to be placed well before the first rain.

Work backwards from the rain

The monsoon in Bangladesh usually runs from June to September, with showers starting earlier. If your umbrellas should reach dealers before the season, they need to be printed, made and delivered before then. Count back from that date, not from the day the rain starts.

Everyone orders at the same time

Demand for umbrellas rises sharply just before the monsoon. Orders placed early get planned calmly. Orders placed at the last minute compete with everyone else's.

Summer counts too

In the hot months people open umbrellas against the sun. A campaign that hands out umbrellas in spring gets used through summer and again through the rains, so the same gift works for two seasons.

A simple plan

  • Fix your quantity and budget first.
  • Finalise the logo and colours next.
  • Place the order and ask us for the current production time.
  • Plan delivery to your dealers or branches before the season opens.

Message us with your target date and we will tell you what is possible.
